Form follows the question

Put related things together.

A short summary preserves facts. A shared timeline makes waiting visible. Graph asks for a form suited to the material, rather than the same page skeleton for every subject.

Interaction earns its place

Change a condition, see why.

At 08:03, walking wins. At 08:17, the ferry wins. The control reveals a dependency; it isn’t here just to move a graphic. A static comparison is enough when the condition is fixed.

Evidence stays in view

Separate facts from the model.

Schedules belong to the source note. Totals come from a calculation. Perfect service is an assumption. Graph keeps those layers distinct; a working slider doesn’t prove a real-world claim.

An authored Graph demonstration. Routes and timings are invented for teaching. The three views are examples, not a mandatory Graph workflow or a model benchmark.
