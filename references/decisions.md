# Design direction

Graph helps readers understand source material while retaining the distinction
between evidence, interpretation, and uncertainty. The subject and audience
guide the language, visual form, interaction, and level of detail.

Composition and implementation are choices for the task. A fixed page
skeleton, palette, chart type, interaction count, or archive format is not a
universal requirement. Use a comparison or operation when it improves
understanding, and complete the selected interactions so their states and
explanations remain clear.

Automated checks can find some document and runtime problems. Literal number
and quotation matches are review hints, not proof of attribution or accuracy.
Review the rendered page, important controls, and source evidence separately.
