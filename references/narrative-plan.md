# Optional outline for complex material

Use a short outline when the material has several dependencies, findings,
or perspectives. Ordinary inputs do not require a separate planning document.

Capture only what helps composition:

- **Reader and goal**: what the reader should understand after visiting.
- **Coverage**: which parts of the source are included and what is inaccessible.
- **Sections**: each section's question, evidence or mechanism, and connection
  to the rest. Include caveats that could change the interpretation.
- **Possible expression**: a diagram, table, chart, equation, example, or
  short explanation that might make the point clear.
- **Optional exploration**: what a reader could usefully inspect or vary,
  and what data or explicit assumptions support the response.
- **Source anchors**: the locations needed to check important claims.

A plan can contain visual terms and implementation ideas. Revise it when
exploring the expression reveals a better explanation. No field requires
inventing a claim, experiment, or state change that the material lacks.
