# Rings and composition

Useful for a small set of parts forming a meaningful whole. Bars or a table
may make close comparisons easier.

- Segment angle represents share of the stated total. Name the unit,
  period, and denominator; explain rounded totals.
- Thin segments need enough contrast and readable outside labels. Avoid
  rounded caps that obscure small segments or change apparent proportions.
- Group categories only when the grouping is meaningful, and identify
  what “other” includes when it matters.
- A total in the center is optional. Count-up, hover, a fixed start angle,
  and a particular palette are presentation choices, not data requirements.
