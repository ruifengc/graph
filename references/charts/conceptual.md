# Glyph · Conceptual (no-statistics inputs)

For inputs with NO measurable data — essays, criticism, philosophy,
talks — where the argument still has STRUCTURE that geometry can
carry. The rule that keeps these honest: **draw only what the source
describes; invent nothing, not even a plausible continuation.**

## Forms

- **Concept curve**: qualitative axes (both may be labeled 定性), the
  curve follows only the source-described segments. If the source says
  "it rose, then fell back", draw exactly that — omitting the classic
  continuation the source never mentions is the point (an invented
  recovery segment is fabrication in curve form). Hover anchors sit at
  the inflection points.
- **Dual-line concept curve**: two qualitative series in one plot when
  the argument IS their relationship (one rides above the other, one
  spikes past, they converge). Same honesty: only described segments.
- **Lineage / taxonomy**: a school or idea branching from shared
  roots — nodes and descent edges, the barrier or break drawn as a
  distinct element.
- **Source-metaphor diagram**: the text's own metaphor drawn literally
  (a coordinate system whose axis dissolves; a self that loses its
  reference frame). Nothing beyond the metaphor.
- **Gate / flip**: converging drive arrows meet a barrier; crossing it
  is the claim (the state on the far side differs in kind, not degree).
- **Mirror**: an entity and its reflection/counterpart — solid ink on
  one side, dashed-accent phantom on the other; the reflection axis is
  the argument.

## Data contract

- Every segment, node, and edge traces to a source statement — if a
  shape can't point at its sentence, it doesn't get drawn.
- Qualitative step/axis heights may be illustrative, but the caption
  says so.
- Hover still applies: anchor points at the shape's meaningful
  positions (inflections, gate, mirror axis), tooltip carries the
  source sentence.

## Caption language (verbatim)

- 「概念示意，非数值数据」
- 「概念曲线，非实测数据」
- 「台阶高度为定性示意，非实测数值」
- 「横轴等距仅为事件顺序示意，非时间比例」

## Constraints

- No metric axes disguised as qualitative ones; label qualitative axes
  as qualitative.
- Never simulate precision the source lacks (no decimal ticks, no
  measured-looking gridlines).
- One accent per chart still applies — the argument's turning point
  gets it (the flip marker, the phantom, the barrier).
