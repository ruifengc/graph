# Workflow · News

Input: a digest / batch of related news items (e.g. collected from RSS
feeds on one topic).

Same seven-step pipeline as `article.md` — the differences:

1. **Cluster the items.** Read all items, group them into 2–5 story
   lines (who did what, what changed). The page is about the *situation
   the batch describes*, not about listing articles.
2. **The claim is the synthesis** — what the batch as a whole shows
   (e.g. "three sub-sectors, three clocks") that no single item shows.
3. **Each story line earns evidence:** its key numbers and dates, each
   with its source article (name + date + URL).
4. **Timeline glyph shines here** — events across items on one axis
   often tell the story better than charts.
5. **Conflicting items are surfaced**, not smoothed over: the page notes
   disagreement in captions ("sources differ on X").

Framework status: first version. Will be enriched by real news runs —
record gaps in runtime notes.
