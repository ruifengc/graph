# Data and statistics

Use with [base.md](base.md). Establish the data's meaning before finding a story.

- Inspect fields, units, coverage, missing values, and collection method.
  A dataset may support an exploration or reference page without one
  striking headline. Do not force a trend or causal explanation.
- Preserve definitions that affect comparisons: nominal/real values,
  cumulative/single-period growth, seasonal adjustment, changing base
  periods, sampling, and population. Keep incompatible measures separate.
- Compute derived quantities from the source data. State denominators and
  assumptions; distinguish missing values from zero. Verify claims such as
  “only negative value” or “lowest since…” against the relevant full series.
- Generate plotted marks, labels, summaries, and tooltips from the same
  data representation where practical. Check consequential calculations
  and data transformations; see [data-cross-check.md](../references/data-cross-check.md).
- Keep visual encodings faithful: length/area/count correspond to the
  quantity claimed; disclose log scales, breaks, aggregation, and sampling.
  Use shared scales for comparable magnitudes or explicitly distinguish them.
- Cite the dataset/release, period, and relevant definition or table.
  Preserve uncertainty and rounding notes that affect the conclusion.
